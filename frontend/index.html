Cyclone risk mapping and scenario analysis

Cyclonex is a research tool for exploring how tropical cyclone scenarios could affect coastal areas. Set a storm's position, wind speed, pressure and motion to see modeled wind and damage across a map.

Open the cyclone dashboard

Explore modeled impacts

Use overview grids for wider areas or select 200 m detail for a bounded area. Inspect a cell to see the assumptions and calculations behind its damage screening index.

Check the source of each result

Cyclonex connects to Open-Meteo for weather and HYCOM for ocean profiles. It labels ocean climatology fallbacks and distinguishes experimental satellite analysis from observed data.
